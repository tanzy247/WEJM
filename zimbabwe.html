Victoria Falls

Day 1 We visited the world's largest sheet of falling water. Located on the Zambezi River between Zambia and Zimbabwe, it spans 1,708 meters wide and drops up to 108 meters.

Day 2 We went white water rafting along the Zambezi River which was great fun. Unfortunately, we don’t have any photos.

We were the only raft however not to capsize so we must have been something right!
