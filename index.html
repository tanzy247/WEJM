South Africa

Sea Point

Sea Point

After an incredible safari we headed to Cape Town for a few chilled days to reset before our next big adventure.

We enjoyed staying in Sea Point where we could walk along the promenade, watch the sunset and enjoy fish and chips - although they were obviously not quite as good as the UK.

Table Top Mountain

Table Top Mountain

We hike to the top of Table Top Mountain where we got great views of the city and peninsula.

Simon’s Town

Simon's Town

We enjoyed visiting Simon’s Town where we spent the morning diving the Kelp Forest. Whilst out on the boat between dives, we were also lucky enough to spot whales jumping out of the water.
In the afternoon we headed to Boulders Beach to spot wild African penguins.

We enjoyed more walks in Sea Point.

We were lucky enough to be in Cape Town when their famous Springbocks were playing their biggest rivals the All Blacks. We headed to the local market to watch the game where there was a fantastic, fun atmosphere.

Robben Island

Robben Island

On our final day in Cape Town, we visited the famous Robben Island where Nelson Mandela was imprisoned for 18 years alongside thousands of others during the apartheid ruling.

The experience was extremely moving. Our tour guide was an ex-prisoner himself and he told us his story of how he was arrested whilst at university under false charges alongside thousands of others.

We were given a full tour of the island including the prison and were talked through the horrific ‘day in the life of’.

This was the exact cell Nelson Mandela lived in for 18 years whilst on Robben Island.

Pakistan

Skardu

Skardu

Arriving in Scardu we had a full day to prepare for our trek, renting necessary equipment as well as exploring Kharpocho fort and Upper Kachura Lake.

Askole

Transfer

The next day we embarked on the 6 hour transfer by Jeep from Skardu to Askole - the village where we would start our trek.

Day 1

Day 1

We were extremely excited to start our trek today! Leaving Askole behind and trekking 20km to Jhola - 3103m.

This is where we were first introduced to our lovely goat mark who would be joining us for the beginning of the trek.

Day 2

Day 2

Day 2 was a long 22km trek to Paiju campsite - 3418m. We walked along the glacial river and got our first views of Trango Tower.

Day 3

Day 3

Day 3 we did an acclimatisation hike and got our first views of broad peak.

Day 4

Day 4

We left Paiju camp behind and trekked 15k to Khoburtse - 3778m. This is when we first started trekking on Baltora glacier.

Each day we had an amazing kitchen team who cooked and provided a hot lunch half way through the trek.

I particularly love this photo as it demonstrates the sheer size of the mountains and how insignificant we are in comparison.

Day 5

Day 5

Thankfully day 5 gave us shorter trek of just 6km to Urdukus - 3905m.

We arrived early and spent the afternoon chilling in the sun, enjoying the views!

Day 6

Day 6

Today we trekked 12k to Goro 2 - 4285m. Here was our first night also sleeping on the glacier.

It was a beautiful day for views. Trekking through icebergs, passing glaciers and getting clear views of G4, G2, G5 and K1.

This is where we said goodbye to Mark the goat.

Day 7

Day 7

We trekked 14k to Concordia - 4512m where we got our first views of K2 as well as Broad Peak, Mitre Peak, Gasherbrum, Sia Kangri and many more. It was a beautiful place to camp.

Day 8

Day 8

Today was an exciting day as we made our way to K2 base camp. First we trekked to Broad Peak Base Camp and then to K2 - 5155m.

When we first arrived at K2 the views were covered by the clouds; however, later in the evening we got a quick clearing and got our first proper view of K2 up close!

Day 9

Day 9

Jade’s birthday! This morning was extremely magical! We woke up to snow and beautiful mountain views. We were the first ones awake at 5am so we got to appreciate the sun coming up over the mountain as K2 was revealed.

After a magical morning we headed back to Concordia where we were surprised with a birthday cake in the middle of the mountains - true genius!

Day 10

Day 10

Today saw us begin our decent back to Askole. We trekked 18km along the glacier to to Goro 1 camp.

We enjoyed our final views of K2 and Broad Peak Base.

Day 11

Day 11

We trekked 18km to Liligo.

Day 12

Day 12

We continue to re-trace our steps. Enjoying the surrounding views, we trekked 18km to Bardumal.

Day 13

Day 13

Today we trekked the final 15km back to Jhola, where we then jumped in a jeep back to Askole.

End of the Trek

End of the Trek

When we arrived back in Askole we had a final meal with the group before departing to Skardu the following day.

In Skardu we received certificates for completing the trek.

Our incredible guides, Kamal and Jamal, made our trip possible.

Guide Photos

Kamal (one of our guides) took some incredible photos along the way.
