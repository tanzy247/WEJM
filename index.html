Tanzania

Mount Kilimanjaro

Day 1

Day 1

Our first venture into Tanzania was to conquer Mount Kilimanjaro - Africa's highest peak at 5,895 meters.

In the morning we met the amazing team who we would be spending the next week with including our guide, assistant guide, chef and porters who make the incredible trip possible.

In the afternoon we arrived at the starting gate and made the 7km hike to the first camp. We were pleased to be greeted at the top by our porter team who had set up camp ready for the night.

Day 2

Day 2

A long day trekking 7km to Shira 1 camp for a lunch stop and then another 10km to Shira 2 camp where we set up camp for the evening.

Day 3

Day 3

Day 3 is an important acclimatisation day. We trekked 7km to the Lava Tower (4,650m) where we stopped for lunch. After reaching this high point, we descended 3km to Barranco Camp for the night.

Day 4

Day 4

We began our ascent up the iconic Barranco Wall. We trekked 5km to Karanga Camp where we slept for the night - above the clouds!

Day 5

Day 5

A shorter trekking day, and we arrived at Barafu Camp by 11:30am - giving us exactly 12 hours before we started our summit hike! We spent the afternoon relaxing and playing cards in preparation for our big day!

Day 6 - Summit Day

Day 6

The day we had been working towards! We woke at 10:30pm (Day 5) and set off at 11:30pm. We trekked through the darkness toward Stella Point on the crater rim. By 6:30am we were able to see the sunrise over Africa. From Stella Point, we trekked for another hour until we finally reached Uhuru Peak (5,895m- the highest point in Africa).

Mike reached the top at 7:08am and Jade reached the top 1 hour later at 8:10am.

After a gruelling long 8hr + hike to the top we had to make the journey back down to Barafu camp where we rested for a few hours before setting off again to Mweka Camp. It was an extremely exhausting day, but we were extremely pleased to have made it safely to the top and achieved our goals.

Day 7

Day 7

Our final day saw us hike 13km down to the final gate where we celebrated with cake and our fantastic team.

Tarangire National Park

Safari - Day 1

Safari

No rest for the wicked! After finally having a proper shower and sleeping in a nice comfy bed for the night, we set off the next morning on our safari.

Day 1 on the safari we visited Tarangire National Park - famous for large elephant herds and iconic baobab trees. An incredible first day seeing giraffes, elephants and 2 lions lazing by the river.

Ngorongoro Crater

Safari - Day 2

Ngorongoro Crater

A new adventure visiting the crater on day 2. It was unbelievable to see the large volumes of wildebeest, buffalo and zebra. We were also lucky to spot ostrich, rhino, flamingo, warthog and 2 lions.

We enjoyed a lovely picnic lunch in the park and then in the afternoon we headed to the Serengeti.

Serengeti

En route

Whilst en route to our accommodation in the Serengeti we managed to have a short game drive and were extremely lucky to find; a cheetah mum with 4 cubs, a pride of lions resting, a hyena and a pregnant cheetah.

We watched the sunset and then made our way to our lodge. A perfect end to an incredible day.

Safari - Day 3

Day 3

After an early breakfast we set off back into the Serengeti in hope to spot more wildlife. We were not disappointed!

First we found lions resting in the shade after they had just killed a buffalo.

Later in the day we were lucky to discover a leopard mum and cub- completing our hunt for the big 5!

As well as giraffe, elephant and more lions!

Safari - Day 4

Day 4

We had an early start this morning. Julie and Yorkie had kindly treated us to a hot air balloon ride - one for our bucket lists! We arrived early so we could catch the sunrise.

We excitedly waited for the balloon to fill and it wasn’t long before we were in the sky flying over the Serengeti

From above we were able to see giraffe, gazelle, elephants, a heard of lions grazing on a buffalo and to top it off we landed next to a cheetah! Incredible!

Landing

Upon landing we were greeted with champagne, a bush breakfast as well as birthday celebrations for Mike.

Game Drive

After a fantastic trip in the hot air balloon, we had time for one final game drive in the Serengeti before making the journey back to Arusha.

We got to enjoy the Serengeti for one last time- spotting lions, hippos and elephants.

Zimbabwe

Victoria Falls

Victoria Falls

Day 1 We visited the world's largest sheet of falling water. Located on the Zambezi River between Zambia and Zimbabwe, it spans 1,708 meters wide and drops up to 108 meters.

Day 2 We went white water rafting along the Zambezi River which was great fun. Unfortunately, we don’t have any photos.

We were the only raft however not to capsize so we must have been something right!
