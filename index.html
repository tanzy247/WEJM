Nepal

Kathmandu

Kathmandu

We first arrived in Kathmandu and had 1 day to organise ourselves ready for the trek. It was so lovely to be back in Nepal and we especially enjoyed tucking into Momos!

The next day we headed from Kathmandu to Bhadrapur where we got our first glimpse of the Himalayas. We were lucky to have a clear day and could spot several of the 8,000m peaks: Everest, Lhotse, Makalu, Kanchenjenga and Cho Oyu.

Bhadrapur

Bhadrapur

When we arrived in Bhadrapur we had a 2-day jeep ride through the mountain to get to our start point. Due to landslides, and broken bridges when we finished our Jeep ride, we also had to trek 4km to Itahari where we reached our tea house for the night.

Kanchenjunga

Day 1

Day 1

Today we hiked 6 hours to Thangyam - 2400m. We arrived at a lovely little tea house where we ate delicious dhal bhat and enjoyed playing cards with Tek and Ram (our guide and porter) as well as chatting with the local tea house owner.

Day 2

Day 2

Today was a nice easy day. We hiked 2 hours through the forest to Gyabla - 2700m. When we arrived, we enjoyed chilling outside and getting our first small glimpse of the mountains to come. We enjoyed eating delicious momos and learning more about how the local people live.

Day 3

Day 3 saw us hike up 800m to Ghunsa. We passed a few small villages and several yaks and cows as well as an apple orchard where we sampled delicious fresh apples. We arrived in the early afternoon and then enjoyed spending the afternoon chilling and playing cards with Tek and Ram.

Day 4

Day 4

Today was a nice and easy rest/acclimatisation day. We took the opportunity to have a nice hot shower - our last chance for the next week.

In the afternoon we enjoyed walking around the small village. We visited the local hospital, school and bakery. We particularly enjoyed playing volleyball with the children on the school field.

In the evening we enjoyed dal bhat with some locals.

Day 5

Day 5

This morning we walked 5hours to Kambachen - 4200m. Unfortunately, it was extremely cloudy when we arrived, so we didn’t get any views of the mountain; however, we were pleased to miss the downpour that followed! We spent the afternoon relaxing in the wood fired dining area where we played cards with Tek and Ram.

Day 6

Day 6

Today we woke up slightly earlier in hope to see the Mountain views before the clouds came in. Thankfully we were not disappointed - we woke to blue skies and fantastic views. We hiked up to Jannu viewpoint, where we got a closer look at the mountains and enjoyed visiting a religious site where local Nepalese hike to pray. We returned in time for lunch just as the clouds came in.

Day 7

Day 7

This morning we woke early again and set off for Lonak - 4800m. We hiked 5 hours and enjoyed the views along the way. Today was the best day so far for Mountain views.

Unbelievably, we had the cutest guest join us for our hike today - a puppy! Our previous guesthouse had been looking after the gorgeous puppy for 2 days until it could be carried up to its new owners. When they first told us we would be carrying the puppy up in a carrier bag I was in disbelief, but the puppy was extremely content and fell asleep on the way up!

Day 8

Day 8

This morning we hiked up to Kanchenjunga north base camp - 5150m. We started early in the morning fog, but as soon as the clouds came up, we had beautiful Mountain views. We hiked 4 hours to the base camp; however, as we arrived the clouds had started to come in. We stayed the night at base camp in hope to see views in the morning.

Day 9

Day 9

We woke to snow up at base camp, which was truly beautiful. We managed to get a glimpse of Kanchenjunga in the early morning before the clouds rolled in. By 8am we made the decision to head back down as the sky was thick with snow.

Day 10

Day 10

Today we made our way back down to Ghunsa. To begin we had some nice views of the mountain but 2 hours in we were struck by a snowstorm. We pushed through the weather and thankfully as we closed into the Ghunsa the weather cleared and we managed to dry out.

Day 11

Day 11

Today we had a rest day in Ghunsa. It was nice to have a shower, rest and reset ready for the south side of the trek.

Day 12

Day 12

We hiked up to Selele High Camp - 4300m. It was a tough 1000m climb, however it was worth it to enjoy some of the views on the way.

Day 13

Day 13

Today was a difficult but rewarding day. We tackled the 3 high passes to Tseram. We were lucky to have clear blue skies for the majority of the day. From the first pass we could see Everest, Makalu and Jannu. We stopped for a tea at the second pass and then quickly made our way to the third pass before the clouds came in. We could see the full Kanchenjunga range.

Day 14

Day 14

Today was one of the most beautiful days of the whole trek! We woke up at 4:30am in hope to get perfect views before the clouds came in - we were not disappointed. We hiked up to South Camp - 4800m and arrived by 9:30am. We enjoyed our breakfast and spent 2 hours at the top enjoying the stunning views. We then made the long 1000m decent back to Tseram.

Day 15

Day 15

Today was our final full day trekking! It’s always bittersweet making your way down knowing the trek is almost over but a shower is in reach!

Indonesia

Raja Ampat

Indonesia

Raja Ampat

After lots of hiking, we headed to Indonesia’s beautiful Raja Ampat for some diving and relaxation.

It took 3 flights and 2 boats to reach our beautiful beach homestay but it was so worth it!

The water was absolutely beautiful. We enjoyed snorkelling and diving daily.

We stayed on 3 different islands during our visit to Raja Ampat: Kri Island, Arborek and Gam.

Piaynemo

Piaynemo

We also did a day trip to Piaynemo which was absolutely beautiful. We enjoyed diving in the morning and visiting the various viewpoints in the afternoon.

Diving

During our 13 dives spread across the trip we saw incredible marine life and coral. Some favourites were:

  • Pygmy seahorse
  • Orangutan crab
  • Eagle ray
  • Spotted ray
  • Reef sharks
  • Turtles
  • Wobbegong shark
  • Nurse Shark
  • Boxer shrimp
  • Clown fish
  • Sea snake
