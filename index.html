Kasane

Chobe National Park

Sunset River Cruise

We started our 8 day self-drive safari in Kasane where we first explored Chobe River Front by boat. We got to enjoy watching elephants and hippos, playing, eating and swimming in the water.

Chobe River Front

Chobe River Front

The next day we headed into the park for opening and explored the national park by land where we were lucky to spot lots of groups of giraffe and elephant.

En Route to Khwai

En Route

Day 3 was a long travel day driving 8 hours to Khwai; however, we continued to spot lots of wildlife including a lion sleeping in the sun. We particularly enjoyed our spot to camp in Khwai as it was home to several elephants.

Khwai

Khwai

Khwai was a quieter day for wildlife due to floods from the Okavango Delta but we still enjoyed exploring and cooking a nice spaghetti bolognaise in the evening.

Savuti

Day 1

Day 1

After Khwai we headed to Savuti which was one of our favourite stops on the self-drive.

As we approached Savuti we spotted a large male leopard where we were able to get extremely close to observe it resting in the shade.

We spent the day game driving the Savuti area where we discovered giraffes and a dead elephant that had been killed by a group of lions and then left for vultures.

Later that evening we discovered a pride of 8 lions.

Day 2

Day 2

We headed out early in hope to see more big cats active… we were not disappointed!

Shortly into our morning drive we discovered a family of lions- 1 male, 1 female and 4 cubs! We were absolutely ecstatic!

After the lions, we continued our game drive and discovered giraffes, zebra, wildebeests, elephants and warthogs. We particularly enjoyed watching the giraffe drinking by the waterhole.

At the end of the day we headed back to Khwai where we set up camp and watched the sunset.

Nxai Pan

In the morning we packed up out tent box and headed to Nxai Pan National Park. As we were leaving Khwai we spotted a group of hyenas feasting on elephant remains.

Nxai Pan National Park

Arriving in Nxai Pan in the late afternoon was an elephant paradise. We spent the whole afternoon watching elephants play in the water, eat and play fight until the sunset.

Maun

Maun

After an incredible trip, it was time to head to Maun where we handed back our vehicle and concluded our trip in Botswana.
